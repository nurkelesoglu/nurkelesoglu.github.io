This line of work combines knowledge graphs with large language models to turn forecasting plots into faithful, accessible natural-language explanations. The companion **User Query Dataset** collects user queries for developing knowledge-graph-grounded multimodal explanations for trustworthy time-series forecasting in DevOps systems.

The approach was presented as *From Plots to Words: Model-Aware Multimodal Explanations as a Foundation for Accessible, Non-Visual Interaction* at **ICMI Companion 2026**.
