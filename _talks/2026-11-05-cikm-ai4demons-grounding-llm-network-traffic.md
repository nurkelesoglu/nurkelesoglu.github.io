Talk at the 1st International Workshop on Artificial Intelligence for Data Engineering and Management of Networked Systems (AI4DEMONS), held in conjunction with the 35th ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM 2026).

The work explores how knowledge graphs can help identify and explain protocol-level hallucinations in LLM-generated network traffic, bringing semantic grounding and trustworthiness into AI-driven network traffic generation.

Co-authors: Łukasz Sobczak, Joanna Domańska.
