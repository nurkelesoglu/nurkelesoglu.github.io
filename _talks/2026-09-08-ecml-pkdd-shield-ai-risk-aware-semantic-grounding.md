Talk at the SHIELD-AI 2026 workshop (Security, Human Awareness, and Risk Mitigation in AI-driven Systems), part of ECML PKDD 2026 – the European Conference on Machine Learning and Principles and Practice of Knowledge Discovery in Databases, held on September 7–11 in Naples, Italy.

The work makes LLM-based robot planning more trustworthy by introducing a risk-aware semantic grounding layer that evaluates ambiguity, hallucination and semantic conflicts before execution, allowing a robot to decide whether to execute, clarify or reject an instruction.

Co-authors: Łukasz Sobczak, Sławomir Nowak.
