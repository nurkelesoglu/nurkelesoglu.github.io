Presented at the 28th ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction (ICMI 2026), held on October 5–9 in Naples, Italy.

This work investigates cultural bias in large language models through the lens of turn-taking. Using parallel corpora, it examines how judgments about conversational behaviour vary across languages and cultural contexts, and asks whether LLMs learn universal patterns of human communication or reproduce assumptions rooted in particular cultures.
