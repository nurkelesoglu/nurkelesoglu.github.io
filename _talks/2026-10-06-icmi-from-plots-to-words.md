Presented at the 28th ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction (ICMI 2026), held on October 5–9 in Naples, Italy.

This work explores how model-aware, multimodal explanations can make visual information more accessible to people who cannot rely on visual interaction.

Co-authors: Łukasz Sobczak, Joanna Domańska.
